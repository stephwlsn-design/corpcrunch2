What “Tier 1 aggregator” means here

Tier 1 is not “rank for every news keyword.” It is reliable discovery across category hubs, original analysis, and syndication-ready packages that other properties cite.

Tier Examples (archetype) SEO signature Corp Crunch fit
Tier 1 Google News / Yahoo / major wires Massive crawl budget, entity graphs, real-time index, brand queries Aspirational ceiling — own niches first
Tier 1.5 Industry vertical aggregators Topic clusters, newsletter + search loop, high returning visitors Primary 12–18 month target
Tier 2 Regional / category publishers Strong clusters, uneven international, thin product SEO Current competitive band to exit

Gaps vs Tier 1.5

  • Thin content volume vs aggregator crawl demand
  • Limited entity / author E-E-A-T depth
  • No News sitemap / Google Publisher Center ops
  • Weak internal linking graph at scale
  • Brand SERP incomplete (Knowledge Panel, sitelinks)
